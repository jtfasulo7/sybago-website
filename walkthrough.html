Who you're dealing with

One person. Who actually answers.

I'm JT. Mechanical engineer by training — I like systems that work and I can't stand things that leak. Now I build customer-acquisition systems for service businesses.

Sybago is small on purpose. You deal with me every time — not an account manager who started last Tuesday, not a call center, not a ticket queue.

I started this because my own feed was wall-to-wall agencies promising to "scale your pipeline" with nothing behind it. I build the whole machine, I run it myself, and I answer my own phone.

The problem

You already know this feeling.

None of this is a work-ethic problem. It's a demand problem — and demand can be built on purpose.

The crew and equipment sit idle between jobs — feast or famine, a few big pours then weeks of quiet.
The homeowners who "want a new driveway someday" never call — nobody's putting the idea in front of them.
Referrals and repeat work are great, but they don't fill a whole season on their own.
Estimates go out, then go cold — no follow-up system turning them into signed jobs.
