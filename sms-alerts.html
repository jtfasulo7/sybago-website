Lead Alert Text Messages

Sybago clients and their team members can opt in below to get an automated text the moment a prospect submits an estimate or booking request through your website. Alerts go only to your own staff — never to your customers.

Example message
Sybago: New website estimate request
Jane Doe — (435) 555-0123
Project: Driveway Replacement
Tear-out: Yes — replacing existing
Size: Medium — ~400–1,000 sq ft
When: ASAP
Where: Cedar City 84720
Reply STOP to opt out, HELP for help.

Support: support@sybago.ai · You can opt out at any time by replying STOP.

Program terms

  • Who can opt in: Sybago clients and team members they designate. This program is not for the client's own customers or prospects.
  • What you'll receive: one text per new lead submitted through your Sybago-built website or funnel, with the prospect's name, phone, and their form answers.
  • Frequency: varies with your lead volume; typically a few messages per week.
  • Cost: Sybago does not charge for alerts. Your carrier's message & data rates may apply.
  • Opt out: reply STOP to any alert. You'll get one confirmation and nothing further.
  • Help: reply HELP or email support@sybago.ai.
  • Privacy: mobile numbers collected for this program are never shared with third parties for marketing. See our Privacy Policy.
