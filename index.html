FAQ

Stuff people ask before they book

So what exactly do you do?
We run the entire path from stranger to booked job. We build and manage your Meta ads, build the page that traffic lands on, wire up an automatic text that fires the moment someone books, and set you up with a dashboard that tracks every lead it produced. Four pieces, one operator, one system.
Who pays for the ad spend?
You do, and it goes straight to Meta from your own ad account in your own name. We never touch it or mark it up. Our fee is separate and covers managing the whole system. We'll talk through what a sensible starting budget looks like for your market on the call.
How is this different from every other ad agency?
Most of them run ads and stop. You get a pile of leads, no page built to convert them, no follow-up, no tracking, and when it doesn't work they blame your sales process. We own the whole chain — so there's nobody to point at but us. Plus you're dealing with one guy who answers his phone, not an account team with turnover.
How fast will I see something?
The system is usually live inside a week. Leads typically start moving in the first couple of weeks after launch. But the first 30 days is also when we're testing creative and audiences to find what your market responds to — the campaigns generally get better, not worse, as they run. If you want guaranteed leads by Friday, everyone promising that is lying to you.
Do I need a website already?
No. If you don't have one, we build the page the ads need. If you do have one, we'll look at it honestly — sometimes it just needs to be optimized to convert paid traffic, sometimes you're better off with a purpose-built page. We'll tell you which on the call instead of selling you a rebuild you don't need.
What if I want to cancel?
You cancel. Month-to-month, no contracts, no exit fee, no 90-day notice nonsense. Your ad account, your page, your domain, and your customer list are yours and you keep them. We'll be sad about it for roughly one afternoon.
What does it cost?
Depends on your market, your trade, and how much of the system you need built from scratch. We go through it on the call — plainly, with a real number, no "packages" theater. If it's not a fit for what you can spend, we'll say that too instead of stringing you along.
Do you work with my trade?
If you sell a service to homeowners or businesses and you can handle more work, probably yes. Junk removal, roofing, HVAC, landscaping, moving, pressure washing, detailing — the machine is the same, the message changes. See the list above.
How are you different from the guys spamming my Instagram DMs?
We don't spam Instagram DMs.
