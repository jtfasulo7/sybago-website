Why a sign-up count can look low

Meta keeps attributing conversions for up to about three days, so today's and yesterday's sign-up counts rise after the fact. Meta also only counts a sign-up it can tie back to an ad interaction — anyone who arrives on a different device, or with tracking off, joins on Skool without ever appearing here. Expect this figure to sit below Skool's own member count.
